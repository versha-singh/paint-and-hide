PAINT
& HIDE

You are a blank white blob in a wooden hut. Paint yourself to match the room, strike a pose and keep still. The seeker is coming.

E opens the paint panel. Pick colours straight off the walls with the eyedropper (F), then paint your body.

You have 60 seconds before the seeker enters, and you can already do the 3 tasks: collect 3 paint pots, strike a pose at the meadow painting, and water the garden. Finish them all and the seeker comes for you. Stay hidden for 60 seconds to win. The gallery has four connected rooms and a fenced garden out the back, so explore the doorways.

Controls: keyboard and mouse, a game controller (left stick move, right stick look, A paint, Y view, X pose), or touch. On a phone, hold it sideways: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, and the buttons on the right do the rest.
